# 👥 User Personas

**Project:** Family Knowledge Vault

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the primary user personas for Family Knowledge Vault.

These personas represent the different types of people who interact with the platform, their motivations, goals, frustrations, and expected behaviors.

The personas guide product decisions, user experience design, feature prioritization, and future roadmap planning.

---

# 2. Persona Overview

Family Knowledge Vault is designed for households rather than individual users.

Within a family, different members have different responsibilities and expectations.

The platform must provide an experience that feels natural for each of them.

Primary personas include:

1. Family Owner
2. Parent / Household Manager
3. Adult Family Member
4. Senior Citizen
5. Child / Teenager
6. Emergency Contact / Caregiver
7. Future Family Member

---

# Persona 1 — Family Owner

## Overview

The Family Owner creates and manages the family's digital home.

They are responsible for organizing important information, inviting members, and ensuring that family knowledge is preserved.

---

## Typical User

- Parent
- Home Owner
- Eldest Child
- Family Administrator

---

## Goals

- Organize important family information.
- Preserve documents safely.
- Ensure every family member has access when needed.
- Build a long-term digital archive.
- Protect family history.

---

## Responsibilities

- Create family workspace.
- Invite members.
- Manage permissions.
- Organize information.
- Configure security.
- Manage subscriptions.

---

## Pain Points

- Documents scattered everywhere.
- Family members asking for the same files repeatedly.
- Losing important paperwork.
- Difficulty managing access.

---

## Success Looks Like

"I know exactly where every important family document is."

---

# Persona 2 — Parent / Household Manager

## Overview

This person manages day-to-day family activities.

Usually responsible for reminders, appointments, bills, school events, warranties, and household organization.

---

## Typical User

- Mother
- Father
- Guardian

---

## Goals

- Never miss important dates.
- Keep track of household assets.
- Organize children's records.
- Manage family schedules.
- Store medical information.

---

## Frequently Uses

- Calendar
- Medical Hub
- Documents
- Home Inventory
- Shared Knowledge

---

## Pain Points

- Forgetting renewal dates.
- Searching through WhatsApp.
- Lost warranties.
- Missed appointments.

---

## Success Looks Like

"I don't have to remember everything anymore."

---

# Persona 3 — Adult Family Member

## Overview

An adult son, daughter, sibling, or spouse who contributes to family knowledge and accesses shared information.

---

## Goals

- Upload documents.
- Access family records.
- Share memories.
- Contribute recipes.
- Help maintain family history.

---

## Frequently Uses

- Search
- Memories
- Recipes
- Documents
- Timeline

---

## Pain Points

- Unsure where important documents are stored.
- Multiple versions of the same file.
- Family information scattered across devices.

---

## Success Looks Like

"I can find what I need within seconds."

---

# Persona 4 — Senior Citizen

## Overview

Older family members who may contribute memories, stories, recipes, and historical knowledge while requiring an extremely simple interface.

---

## Goals

- Preserve life experiences.
- Share stories.
- Save family recipes.
- View family photographs.
- Stay connected.

---

## Frequently Uses

- Memories
- Recipes
- Timeline
- Family Tree

---

## Pain Points

- Technology feels complicated.
- Fear of making mistakes.
- Small text.
- Too many options.

---

## Design Considerations

- Large buttons
- Simple navigation
- Minimal screens
- Voice input
- High readability

---

## Success Looks Like

"My memories won't be forgotten."

---

# Persona 5 — Child / Teenager

## Overview

Young family members primarily consume family content while occasionally contributing memories and photos.

---

## Goals

- Learn family history.
- View memories.
- Explore the family timeline.
- Share school achievements.
- Preserve personal milestones.

---

## Frequently Uses

- Timeline
- Photos
- Memories
- Family Tree

---

## Pain Points

- Doesn't know family history.
- Information exists only in conversations.

---

## Success Looks Like

"I know my family's story."

---

# Persona 6 — Emergency Contact / Caregiver

## Overview

A trusted person who may temporarily access critical family information during emergencies.

---

## Goals

- Quickly access essential information.
- Contact doctors.
- Find insurance.
- View medical history.
- Retrieve emergency documents.

---

## Frequently Uses

- Emergency Mode
- Medical Hub
- Contacts

---

## Pain Points

- Information difficult to locate during stressful situations.
- Lack of access permissions.

---

## Success Looks Like

"I found everything I needed immediately."

---

- Read stories.

- Understand traditions.
- Preserve legacy.

---

## Frequently Uses

- Timeline
- Memories
- Recipes

| Family Owner | Organization | Family, Documents, Security |

---

# 4. Shared Expectations

Regardless of age or technical ability, every user expects the platform to be:

- Easy to understand.
- Fast.
- Secure.
- Reliable.
- Private.
- Accessible.
- Mobile-friendly.
- Trustworthy.

---

# 5. Design Implications

These personas influence every design decision.

The platform should:

- Prioritize simplicity over complexity.
- Hide advanced functionality unless needed.
- Minimize the number of clicks.
- Use natural language whenever possible.
- Support multiple generations within the same household.
- Make family collaboration effortless.

---

# 6. Future Personas

As the platform evolves, additional personas may emerge.

Examples include:

- Family Historian
- Estate Planner
- Healthcare Professional
- Financial Advisor
- Legal Advisor
- Genealogist

These personas are currently outside the scope of the initial product.

---

# 7. Success Criteria

The personas will be considered successfully supported when:

- Every family member can comfortably use the platform.
- Non-technical users require little to no training.
- Information can be found quickly regardless of who is searching.
- Families actively contribute to preserving knowledge together.
- The platform becomes a trusted part of everyday family life.
