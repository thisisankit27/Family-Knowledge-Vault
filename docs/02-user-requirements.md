# 📋 User Requirement Specification (URS)

**Project:** Family Knowledge Vault

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

---

# 1. Purpose

This document captures the business requirements of Family Knowledge Vault from the perspective of its users.

The goal is to understand **what users need**, rather than **how the system will be implemented**.

These requirements serve as the foundation for product planning, UI/UX design, system architecture, and software development.

---

# 2. Product Overview

Family Knowledge Vault is a digital home where families can securely preserve, organize, search, and share everything that matters.

Unlike traditional cloud storage platforms, the application focuses on preserving relationships, memories, documents, household knowledge, and family history in one connected ecosystem.

---

# 3. Target Users

The platform is intended for:

- Individuals
- Couples
- Families
- Parents
- Children
- Senior citizens
- Caregivers
- Joint families
- Future generations

---

# 4. Business Objectives

The platform should enable families to:

- Never lose important information.
- Preserve memories across generations.
- Organize household knowledge.
- Reduce the stress of finding important documents.
- Improve emergency preparedness.
- Simplify family collaboration.
- Create a permanent digital home for family history.

---

# 5. User Requirement Categories

The requirements are grouped by business capability.

---

# 5.1 Family Management

### UR-001

Users shall be able to create a family workspace.

---

### UR-002

Users shall be able to invite family members.

---

### UR-003

Users shall be able to accept or decline invitations.

---

### UR-004

Family owners shall be able to manage member roles and permissions.

---

### UR-005

Each family shall have its own independent and secure workspace.

---

# 5.2 Family Members

### UR-006

Users shall be able to create profiles for family members.

---

### UR-007

Family member profiles should contain personal information such as:

- Name
- Photo
- Date of Birth
- Contact Information
- Relationships
- Blood Group
- Emergency Contacts

---

### UR-008

Users shall be able to record important life events for each family member.

---

### UR-009

Users shall be able to associate documents, memories, medical records, and assets with a family member.

---

# 5.3 Document Management

### UR-010

Users shall be able to upload important family documents.

---

### UR-011

Documents shall be organized by category instead of folders.

Examples include:

- Identity
- Finance
- Property
- Health
- Education
- Legal

---

### UR-012

Users shall be able to search documents using natural language.

---

### UR-013

Users shall be able to preview documents without downloading them.

---

### UR-014

Users shall be able to securely share documents with authorized family members.

---

# 5.4 Medical Records

### UR-015

Users shall be able to maintain medical records for each family member.

---

### UR-016

Medical history should include:

- Reports
- Prescriptions
- Vaccinations
- Allergies
- Medicines
- Doctors

---

### UR-017

The platform should remind users about recurring medical events.

Examples:

- Vaccinations
- Health Checkups
- Medicine Renewals

---

# 5.5 Memories

### UR-018

Users shall be able to preserve family memories.

---

### UR-019

Memories may contain:

- Photos
- Videos
- Voice Notes
- Written Stories

---

### UR-020

Users shall be able to organize memories by people, places, and events.

---

### UR-021

Users shall be able to view memories on a chronological timeline.

---

# 5.6 Family Recipes

### UR-022

Users shall be able to preserve traditional family recipes.

---

### UR-023

Recipes should support:

- Photos
- Videos
- Voice Recordings
- Ingredients
- Instructions
- Stories

---

# 5.7 Home Inventory

### UR-024

Users shall be able to manage household assets.

---

### UR-025

Assets may include:

- Appliances
- Electronics
- Furniture
- Vehicles
- Valuable Items

---

### UR-026

Each asset should support warranty information, invoices, manuals, and maintenance history.

---

# 5.8 Calendar & Reminders

### UR-027

Users shall receive reminders for important family events.

Examples:

- Birthdays
- Anniversaries
- Bill Payments
- Insurance Renewals
- Property Taxes
- Service Schedules

---

# 5.9 Shared Household Knowledge

### UR-028

Families shall be able to maintain commonly used household information.

Examples:

- Wi-Fi Password
- Service Providers
- Emergency Contacts
- Vendors
- Utility Information

---

# 5.10 Search

### UR-029

Users shall be able to search the entire family workspace from a single search interface.

---

### UR-030

Search results should include every relevant type of information.

Examples:

- Documents
- People
- Memories
- Recipes
- Medical Records
- Inventory

---

# 5.11 Artificial Intelligence

### UR-031

Users shall be able to ask questions using natural language.

Examples:

- Where is Dad's passport?
- Show Mom's insurance policy.
- When was our refrigerator purchased?

---

### UR-032

The system should automatically organize uploaded information wherever possible.

---

### UR-033

The system should assist users without replacing their decision-making.

---

# 5.12 Emergency Mode

### UR-034

Users shall have access to a dedicated emergency dashboard.

---

### UR-035

Emergency Mode should provide quick access to critical information.

Examples:

- Blood Groups
- Emergency Contacts
- Doctors
- Medical Information
- Insurance
- Essential Documents

---

# 5.13 Digital Legacy

### UR-036

Users shall be able to preserve information for future generations.

---

### UR-037

Users shall be able to prepare private instructions, letters, and messages.

---

### UR-038

Legacy information shall remain private until explicitly shared according to user-defined rules.

---

# 5.14 Security & Privacy

### UR-039

Users shall always retain ownership of their data.

---

### UR-040

Users shall have complete control over who can access their information.

---

### UR-041

The platform shall prioritize privacy and security above convenience.

---

# 6. Out of Scope

The following features are intentionally excluded from the initial release:

- Banking transactions
- Social networking
- Public profile sharing
- Public family trees
- AI-generated personal decisions
- Medical diagnosis
- Financial investment advice

---

# 7. Success Criteria

The product will be considered successful when users can:

- Preserve their family's important knowledge.
- Find any important information within seconds.
- Collaborate securely with family members.
- Reduce dependency on paper documents.
- Preserve memories across generations.
- Feel confident that their family's digital home is secure.
