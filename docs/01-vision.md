# 🏡 Family Knowledge Vault

> **A digital home that preserves everything a family knows, owns, celebrates, and wants to pass on.**

---

# Vision

Most software helps us manage work.

Very little software helps us preserve what matters most—our families.

Every household accumulates thousands of pieces of information over a lifetime:

* Important documents
* Medical records
* Insurance policies
* Property papers
* Recipes
* Family photos
* Voice recordings
* Memories
* Home inventory
* Emergency information
* Financial records
* Family traditions

Today, this information is scattered across:

* Google Drive
* WhatsApp
* Email
* Filing cabinets
* Phones
* Laptops
* People's memories

When someone needs something urgently, finding it becomes difficult.

**Family Knowledge Vault aims to become the single trusted place where every family's important knowledge lives—organized, searchable, secure, and preserved for generations.**

---

# The Philosophy

This is **not** another cloud storage application.

This is **not** another AI chatbot.

This is **not** another password manager.

This is a **Family Operating System**.

Instead of storing files...

It preserves a family's entire story.

# Security & Privacy

This product should earn trust before it earns revenue.

Core principles:

* End-to-end encryption where appropriate
* Role-based family access
* Fine-grained permissions
* Secure sharing
* Audit logs
* Backup & recovery
* Multi-factor authentication
* Data export
* Privacy-first architecture

Users should always own their data.
